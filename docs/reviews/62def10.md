## R23 - high: operator writes can enter an already captured decision

Locations: src/sentinel/service.py:279-295, 316-341; ingestion takes its account lock and captures now at 177-184.

The profile query ignores provisioned_at; the disclosure query checks disclosed_at but not recorded_at. Both operator writers commit on independent connections without participating in ingestion's account lock. Under READ COMMITTED, an operator update committed after ingestion captured its cutoff can appear in a later scoring query. An externally backdated disclosure is not proof it was available to this service at the earlier cutoff. The recorded features_as_of then overstates what was knowable.

An independent fresh-Postgres probe paused ingestion immediately before the real _model_score, after its clock was captured, and committed real operator writes on a second connection. The actual registered model and unmodified feature function then scored:

The owned loopback cluster was stopped and its state removed. Reviewer probe: E:/PHANI/Interview/tmp/c07_timing_review_probe.py (local evidence, not a shipped test).
