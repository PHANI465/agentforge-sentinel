## R47 — High: incomplete human sheets manufacture answers and change the protocol

Locations: `scripts/agent_eval_score.py:143` (`read_sheet`) and `:159` (`human_metrics`).

  fields blank, produces `human_correction = 0/1` and `human_acceptable = 0/1`.
  Nobody supplied those negative answers.

  explicit correction is ignored.

unanswered counts/denominators per metric and label partial review honestly. Blank is
unknown, not no; do not call a partially answered packet a completed review. Keep the
blank-template behavior, inert/formula-safe text and separate machine/human metrics.
