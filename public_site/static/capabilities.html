What Sentinel checks

A review flag points to a discrepancy in the supplied evidence. A passed check covers only its stated calculation or comparison. Inconclusive and not-run results identify checks that need more evidence.

Supported evidence

Use searchable PDFs, XLSX sheets or DOCX body tables. Scans need a text layer. For comparisons, use explicit record roles and references. The samples show the supported layout.
